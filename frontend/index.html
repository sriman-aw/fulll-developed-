HOW IT WORKS

One assessment, four things it unlocks.

A student's skill profile is the record everything else reads from — what they're recommended, who can find them, and what they can prove they've done.

01

Assess

A questionnaire and aptitude check scores technical and soft skills against current industry benchmarks.

02

Map

The gap between a student's profile and their target role becomes a ranked list of courses, certifications, and roles.

03

Match

Internships, FDPs, and placements are ranked by fit, not just by date posted — for students and academicians alike.

04

Prove

Completed work lands in a verified portfolio institutions and recruiters can check without a phone call.

PLATFORM

Built to hold four roles without four separate systems.

ACCESS

Role-based by default

A student, an academician, a recruiter, and an institution admin see the same underlying record — but only the fields their role needs, editable only where their role permits.

RECORDS

Secure document vault

Resumes, certificates, internship reports, and transcripts, verified once and reused everywhere on the platform.

COLLAB

Mentorship & live projects

Guest lectures, innovation challenges, and industry-sponsored live projects are scheduled and tracked the same way an internship is.

CONNECT

Works with what you already use

Syncs with certification providers and institutional academic databases — skill records don't need re-entry each term.

INSIGHT

Analytics that reach policy

Placement outcomes and skill-demand trends roll up to a level institutions and policymakers can actually act on.
